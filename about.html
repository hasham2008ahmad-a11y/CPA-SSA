About Us

Our Mission

The CPA Schulich Student Association exists to prepare Schulich students for careers in accounting and the CPA profession. We connect students with mentors, employers, and each other, and build a community around the CPA PEP pathway from first year onward.

$10 a year All years welcome Student-led
Your Provincial Body
CPA Ontario

CPA Schulich is one campus in CPA Ontario's Post-Secondary Ambassador Program — the provincial network building student pathways into Ontario's growing CPA profession.

What We Do

Three things the association focuses on, all year round.

Professional Development

Workshops on resumes, interviews, and the CPA PEP pathway.

Networking

Direct access to firms, alumni, and CPA members.

Community

Social events and a peer network across all years.

Who We Serve

Every Schulich student — whatever year you're in, whatever you're specializing in, and whether or not you've decided on the CPA yet.
