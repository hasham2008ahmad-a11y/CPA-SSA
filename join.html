How to Join

What You Get

One registration, two layers of support — province-wide through CPA Ontario, and on the ground at Schulich.

Through CPA Ontario

  • Networking events with practising CPAs and recruiters
  • Skills workshops, competitions and firm visits
  • Self-paced skills modules, each with a LinkedIn badge
  • The CPA Ontario job board and co-op hour tracker
  • Eligibility to apply for the Board of Ambassadors

Through CPA Schulich

  • Campus events, socials and our own case competition
  • Peer mentorship from upper-year students
  • Resume and interview prep before recruiting season
  • First-year reps whose whole job is helping you settle in
  • A direct line to students who've already done it

What It Costs

PSAP Membership

$10 per year

Paid once a year directly to CPA Ontario when you register. It covers the full provincial calendar of events and the member portal.
CPA Schulich Membership

Free

There's no separate campus fee. Once you're a PSAP member, contact the Campus Team and we'll add you at no cost.

FAQ

Do I need to be an Accounting major?

No. CPA Schulich is open to every Schulich student, whatever your specialization, and PSAP is open to any undergraduate at a Canadian college or university.

Can first-years join?

Yes, and it's the best time to start. We have First Year Representatives on the Campus Team specifically to help you find your footing, and several PSAP events are built for 1st- and 2nd-year students.

I already registered for PSAP — what now?

Just email the Campus Team and we'll add you to the CPA Schulich member list. You don't need to register twice.

What do I get access to right away?

The PSAP member portal, which holds the skills modules, event recordings, the co-op and internship hour tracker, and the CPA Ontario job board. See what's in there.

Can I get more involved than just attending?

Yes. Active PSAP members can apply to CPA Ontario's Board of Ambassadors, and Campus Team roles at CPA Schulich open on a yearly cycle. Being a member is the prerequisite for both.
