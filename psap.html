CPA Ontario Program

Your CPA Career Starts Long Before Graduation

CPA Ontario's Post-Secondary Ambassador Program gives Ontario college and university students a $10-a-year backstage pass to the accounting and business profession — real networking, real workshops, and a direct line to the firms that will eventually be hiring you.

Good grades open doors. What gets you through them is everything you build outside the classroom — the people you know, the way you carry yourself in a room, and whether you can explain, clearly and confidently, why you're worth hiring. PSAP exists to help you build all three, years before you're competing for your first internship.

What Is PSAP?

The Post-Secondary Ambassador Program is CPA Ontario's membership community for undergraduate students at Ontario colleges and universities. For $10 a year, members get access to a full calendar of networking events, skills workshops, competitions, and firm visits — most of which are invitation-only and unavailable to non-members.

It isn't only for students who've already decided to become a CPA. It's aimed just as much at students who are still figuring out what a business career could look like, and want low-pressure exposure to the people already living it.

Who It's For
Any undergraduate at a Canadian college or university — not limited to accounting or business majors.
Cost
$10 per year.
Where It Happens
A mix of virtual sessions and in-person events across Ontario — Toronto, London, Kitchener-Waterloo, Ottawa, and more.

Why Students Join

Build a network that outlasts your degree. Meet practicing CPAs, campus recruiters, and fellow student leaders from schools across Ontario — the kind of connections that turn into mentorships, referrals, and job offers.

Practice the skills employers actually screen for. Workshops focused on communication, critical thinking, and problem-solving — the soft skills that are hardest to prove on a transcript.

Add real substance to your resume. Event planning, leadership roles, and competition results give you concrete things to talk about in interviews, not just coursework.

Join a community, not just a mailing list. PSAP members attend events together, and many describe it as the first place they found a peer group that took their career seriously.

Grow into a leadership role. Active members can apply to the Board of Ambassadors — CPA Ontario's student leadership body — and go from attending events to running them.

The PSAP Journey

Most students join PSAP unsure of a few things: how to walk into a room full of strangers and actually network, how to turn what they're good at into a career story, and whether accounting is even the right path for them.

By the time members go through a few events, the pattern CPA Ontario sees again and again is the same: students who used to hang back at the edge of a networking event are the ones leading a session at the next one. That shift — from unsure to confident, from anonymous to known — is really the entire point of the program.

Three Ways PSAP Shows Up for You

Inspire

Hear directly from CPAs working across industries — audit, tax, government, consulting, entrepreneurship — at conferences and events built to show you what the designation can actually lead to.

Develop

Workshops and competitions built around the skills firms say new grads are missing: case analysis, decision-making under pressure, and clear communication.

Connect

Firm visits, networking nights, and flagship events like CareerFest exist for one reason — to put you in the room with the people who do the hiring.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be an accounting or business student to join?

No. PSAP is open to undergraduates in any program at a Canadian college or university.

Is $10 really the only cost?

That covers the annual membership. Some individual events may have their own costs or capacity limits, so check each event page.

I'm only in first year — is it too early?

It's arguably the ideal time. Several events, including the Fall Case Competition, are built specifically for 1st- and 2nd-year students, and starting early means more time to build the network before you're job-hunting.

What happens after I sign up?

You'll get access to the event portal, where you can register for individual sessions as they open throughout the year.

How do I get more involved than just attending events?

Apply to CPA Ontario's Board of Ambassadors once applications open for the year — it's the leadership track built directly on top of PSAP membership. Schulich holds two seats, both on our Campus Team.
