About the Board of Ambassadors
CPA Ontario’s Board of Ambassadors is the provincial student leadership body — top undergraduates representing more than 20 college and university campuses across Ontario. Our two Co-Presidents hold Schulich’s seats. Applications open on a yearly cycle, and active PSAP members are the ones eligible to apply.
